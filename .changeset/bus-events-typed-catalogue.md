Bus event payloads are declared in one catalogue, `EVENT_PAYLOADS` in `scripts/core/events.ts`; `contract/factory.json` measures its writers against it and reads all 112 event types complete, `card.admitted` with the keys `appendCardEvent` adds. A lifted launch stop re-admits the card from its last admission, keeping only the keys the catalogue declares, so an admission written before the catalogue (with `textVerified` or `bodyMatchesAdmission`) is read instead of throwing.
