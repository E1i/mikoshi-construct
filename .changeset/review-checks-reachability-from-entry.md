Review: the bus walks the relative imports from every entry point of a pull request's head before the review session and records `changes`, with the reason, when a new product module is reached by no entry point of its package. The reviewer reads that finding in its prompt and cannot clear it. Detect and review share one entry-point walker, and template payloads, scripts under a dot-directory and root `*.config.*` files no longer count as product source, at review or at intake.
