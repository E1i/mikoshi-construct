cli: `construct intake` adds a `seam: volume` line to a card whose touches hold more reading than one session context leaves room for (about 100000 tokens, counted from the size of every file the touches name). It proposes `slice: N volume` lines that each fit, keeping a generated file with its sources and a template with its twin, and holds the card for a person to confirm the slices or keep it whole.
