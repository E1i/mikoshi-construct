The owner wait a card opens after an empty answer round now closes when one of the exits its own text names has happened: a new head on the card's pull request, an amend of the card, or a decision bound to it. Before, a new head only let the next answer round start and left the wait open, so the merge of that head was denied `owner_question_open` on a question its own text called lifted. A decision wait of the card opened by any other path is left open.
