`construct intake` refuses a draft in which two cards carry the same task text, gives every slice it cuts a goal line of its own, and makes a card that imports what another card of the draft changes depend on it. A witness can state its kind as a field, `{ "witness": …, "kind": "acceptance" | "invariant" }`, and the downgrade check reads that field before the phrase "red on main". A draft can name a `plan` whose file sits inside the repository and holds a slice table; every card then names its `item`, and a bus intake opens the plan, adds the items and links the cards. A refusal says how to add the missing table.
