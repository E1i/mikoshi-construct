Board bus panel: the bus moves out of the Miko panel into its own full-width panel under it. The header shows the bus version the database carries (`BUS LIVE V<n>`), the age of the last `netwatch.tick` pulse, the last event id and the age of the last published board snapshot. EVENT FLOW shows events per minute now and 30 one-minute bars. TOP EVENTS · 30 MIN shows the share of the four busiest event-type families. NODE HEALTH has one green, yellow or red row each for REDUCER (lag in events behind the in-order watermark), SNAPSHOT (red past two publish intervals), NETWATCH (GitHub `rate_remaining`), WORKERS (alive out of the processes that heartbeat), WAITS (open owner waits) and ALARMS (the newest uncleared `board.alarm` and its card). SLOTS has one segmented bar per queue, yellow when the queue is full. On refresh the page compares the old and new slot readings: a newly taken segment fades in with a slow flicker, and a freed one blinks a few times before it goes dark. RECENT ACTIVITY lists the last four events that are not heartbeats or ticks. If no pulse arrives within two pulse intervals, the panel turns red, reads `BUS DOWN`, and replaces its body with a FLATLINED stamp, the last event and the command that starts the bus. Every figure comes from the bus database.
