A factory's next card number follows its own row. `pnpm bus:request numbers.foreign --ids <list>` records card ids that came from another factory's row, and intake and the round-limit recut then number after the highest id not so recorded and the factory's pull requests and issues. A number never lands on a card the bus already holds, and the recorded cards stay as they are. A bus with no such record numbers as it did before.
