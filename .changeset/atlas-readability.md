cli: the `construct atlas` map reads as a selection, a filter and a search. A selected node lights its edges and neighbours and dims the rest. Each edge kind can be hidden from the legend and shown again, and a note names every hidden kind. A folded group or contour keeps its `bypass` or `unknown` mark under every filter. Search finds contours, parts and entry points as well as files, and opens and centres what it finds. The parts inside a contour sort by name, edge count or state. Edge labels show on hover, on the selection and when zoomed in. On a narrow page the side panel floats over the map
