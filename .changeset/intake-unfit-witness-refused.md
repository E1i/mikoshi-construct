The witness node now records a witness that cannot fail on the base as `unfit`, not green: a witness whose run on the base finds no test, or that names a file absent on the base and outside the card's touches, creates and diff. The card stops with a proposed replacement — the test files from the card's diff, red on main. `construct intake` takes replacing an unfit witness with a red-on-main one as a refinement, not a downgrade, and reads the owner's standing general decisions in force: under one that says a witness checking paths the card itself creates is a witness of the change, such a witness is replaced without an owner wait.
