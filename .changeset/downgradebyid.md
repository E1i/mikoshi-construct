`construct intake --admit` addresses a prose witness by its position, as it does a field witness: the wait for a witness downgrade prints `разрешить понижение свидетеля #N W2` instead of the witness sentence. One decision may name several witnesses: `разрешить понижение свидетелей #N W1 W2` allows those it names and leaves the card refused for the rest. A decision naming a prose witness by its text still lifts its wait.
