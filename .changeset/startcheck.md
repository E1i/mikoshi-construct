`construct doctor --factory` says, before the factory starts, whether it will go: for each item it needs — the files it reads, the eddies and heartbeat hooks in `.claude/settings.json`, pnpm pinned at or above the mikoshi floor, `.pnpm-store/` ignored, a fresh window heartbeat on the bus, and `allow-live merge` and `allow-live update` in force — it prints goes or does not go, and under each red item the command that turns it green. It exits `1` while any item is red; `--json` prints the same checklist.
