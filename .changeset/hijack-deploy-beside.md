attach accepts a repository that carries its own construct, such as a clone of a constructed repository at a tag: the carriers its construct already tracks stay its own and are neither written nor recorded, where before attach refused with "already carries a construct". attach now deploys the factory beside the repository, the directory `~/.construct/<repository id>/` with an empty bus, so the factory started there keeps its bus apart from the machine bus, and records it as `factoryBeside` in `.construct/attach.json`. detach removes that directory and the Engram `construct atlas` wrote for the repository under `~/.construct/engram/`, and nothing else outside the repository; it refuses, removing nothing, while a plist in the user's `Library/LaunchAgents` names that directory, since launchd would raise the factory again, or while a plist there cannot be read. detach writes nothing into `.git` beyond its exclude block: a card branch the factory cut, and its worktree record, stay the user's. A factory started beside a repository keeps its launch logs and session reports in that directory, so detach removes them with it, and `construct atlas` on an attached repository that carries its own construct writes its Engram under `~/.construct/engram/`, leaving the tracked `construct.model.json` as it was. Whether a factory process still runs is not detach's judgement: stop the factory before detaching it.
