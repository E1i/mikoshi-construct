The law registry no longer has a cell that names a merged card. Six cells now have a witness that calls the code of its own node, measured by V8 coverage: Z2 × answer, Z2 × launch, Z2 × update, Z3 × answer, Z4 × answer and Z5 × launch. The answer witnesses run through `AnswerNode` and the update witness through `UpdateNode`, not their executors. The launch witnesses read the handoff and the report through `launch-root.ts`. Their gap rows leave `contract/ratchet.json`. No merged card's witness (#1672, #1754, #1764, #1767, #1789) calls its node, so each cell that named one of those cards, and each cell still waiting for a witness, now names the open card #1823, law-cells-40-witnesses. The Z4 cells keep #1740 beside it, and Z3 × merge names #1784. The frozen list of 31 cells that were allowed to name a merged card is gone. A single list of the closed cards judged today, this card included, now decides whether a gap is legal, and the law-sites suite checks that no cell names a card on it. The laws section of the factory contract moves to version 3.
