The board page has a UI version of its own, shown as `ui v4` in the header in place of the package version. Clicking the WARNINGS tag folds the warnings to the tag, which then shows their count inside a slowly glowing red-to-amber border, and the board remembers the choice per viewer. A session card that joins or leaves SERVICES glitches in or out while the grid eases to its new height. Hovering or focusing a daemon slot shows the daemon's purpose from its skill's description, or "no description". The depends and state columns of the card table wrap instead of growing wide.
