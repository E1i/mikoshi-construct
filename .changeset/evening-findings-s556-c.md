One supervision for card, answer and review sessions. A window start ends with its window session, so a later takeover is no longer refused by a start that never ends. `bus:run` sweeps the leased launch, answer and review tasks: a launch or answer session with no push and no context change past 30 minutes is closed and queued again when its process is gone, and raises one `session.stalled` event with its reason while it lives. A session that reaches its Eddies spend limit is stopped with a `session.stalled` event naming the spend and the limit. A review session whose context has not grown for 8 minutes is ended there, before its 20-minute ceiling, even while its heartbeat goes on. A restart that drains a held review lease retires that worker, and the new one takes the free review slots. The pid check of a recorded worker moved from `review-bg.ts` to `recorded-worker.ts`, and the launch node reads a card session whose recorded pid is gone as ended. An answer round merges main into the card tree before its session through the update node's merge, and a conflicting merge leaves the tree as it was and goes the update node's conflict path. A launch task stopped by fault on a tree with uncommitted changes comes back with `requeue --task <key> --keep-tree`, which launches the session on that tree as it stands, and a plain requeue refused on that cause names this step.
