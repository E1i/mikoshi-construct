The intake grammar symbols `ADMIT_SOURCE`, `LANE_FILE` and `isLaneName` move from `src/commands/intake/admit.ts` to `src/card/grammar.ts`, and `pathsNamedOutside` becomes private to `scripts/core/card-bodies.ts`, its only reader. `busCardStates` moves from `scripts/core/tasks.ts` to `scripts/core/card-readings.ts`, and so do `lastHeartbeat` and `LastHeartbeat` from `scripts/supervisor/heartbeat.ts`. `retirePreviousGeneration` and `pidOfProcess` move to `scripts/core/db.ts`. Where the old file's edge to the new owner points down, it re-exports the moved symbol. The declarations move `scripts/intake/lane-json.ts` and `scripts/intake/parking-reads.ts` into card-record, `src/card/closed.ts` into card-model, and `scripts/intake/witness-fields-backfill.ts` into session. The backfill now reads card bodies through `cardBodyTexts` instead of querying the card-record table itself. The ratchet rows `card-record->rules`, `card-record->session` and `quality->intake` fall to none, and `card-record->intake`, `card-record->quality`, `decisions->session` and `ports->intake` fall. The rows `ports->card-record`, `session->intake`, `session->quality` and `session->rules` take only imports that existed at the base and moved with their files.
